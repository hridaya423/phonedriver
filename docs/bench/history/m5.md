## Verdict

Met on both devices: screenshot (285 and 332 ms against 400), snapshot of Settings (264 and 254 ms against 300), tap to settled diff (1167 and 1053 ms against 1200). Missed: tap (534 ms iPhone, 454 ms iPad against 80) and, on the iPad by 4 ms, typing 100 characters (404 ms against 400; iPhone 386 ms met).

Limiter for the tap miss: the runner waits for XCTest's event completion (about 230 ms after the app has the touch, decision D15), and a device adds the USB round trip and the settle poll's screenshot on top, 175 ms above the Simulator's 359 ms on the iPhone and 95 ms above it on the iPad. The testbed's own log shows the touch reaching the app within 25 ms of the request (touch began and ended 25 ms apart), so the budget is spent in XCTest's completion and the runner's reply, not in delivery. Not profiled further; this repeats D15 on hardware.

The scenario suite (12/12) was not run on the devices: Phase 5's scenarios are driven for the Simulator.
